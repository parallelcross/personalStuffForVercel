# Wardrobe studio design direction

The selected direction is a quiet editorial spread followed by a catalog-style six-person outfit board. Three private image compositions were explored: editorial spread, catalog and stylist board. The editorial spread gave the clearest family view; the catalog contributed direct individual selection. The stylist board was rejected because surrounding garments competed with faces and required too much rearrangement on phones.

Retain Cormorant Garamond and Manrope, ivory surfaces, restrained borders and the three structured palettes. Keep the complete family visible without image cropping. A portrait board is not a scale chart: each individual study is separately framed. Always label generated images as approximate AI wardrobe visualizations.

Public mode now includes the reviewed, approved generated family visualizations and individual studies, alongside original shopping illustrations. Original photos and design mockups remain local. Approved copies are recorded in content/published-assets.json; do not introduce private asset imports.

Concept switches are immediate to support side-by-side visual judgment and keyboard responsiveness. Avoid double-exposed faces during crossfades. Reduced-motion support remains global. Selection is indicated with a persistent border and text, never color alone.
