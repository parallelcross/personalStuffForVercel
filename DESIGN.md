# Design System: Wear Family Photo Outfit Selection

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Quiet editorial spread"**

The wardrobe studio pairs a quiet editorial spread with a catalog-style six-person outfit board. Warm paper, expressive serif titles, practical sans-serif details and restrained rules let the approved family imagery carry the visual richness. The five wardrobe concepts keep distinct clothing palettes within one consistent interface.

The original composition studies established the editorial family view and direct individual selection. Surrounding garments from the rejected stylist-board study competed with faces and required too much rearrangement on phones. Preserve the complete family image without cropping; separately framed individual studies are not a scale chart.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Image-led editorial composition with open paper fields.
- Substantial serif names and clear sans-serif shopping details.
- Quiet boundaries, persistent selection and immediate concept switching.

This record merges the approved typography/color refinement into the incumbent direction. The normative values above are extracted from `src/tokens.css` and `src/typography.css`; layout and state behavior come from `src/style.css`. `src/main.tsx` supplies the font files and `index.html` the initial font preloads and paper-colored browser theme. This is a screen system; the existing print stylesheet keeps its separate point sizes.

## Colors

Warm paper and charcoal support a restrained evergreen action color. These semantic interface colors stay separate from clothing colors in `content/wardrobe.json`.

### Primary

- **Evergreen** (`accent`): links, progress, selected filter borders, focus and the recommendation field.
- **Deep evergreen** (`accent-hover`): pointer-hover links and the dark button hover.
- **Quiet green wash** (`hover`): pointer-hover choices.
- **Selected wash** (`selected`): the base outfit-choice state; the current portrait board remains transparent and carries selection with its inset underline.

### Secondary

- **Clay caution** (`attention`): explicit shopping cautions. Routine price-status text is muted; observed price-status text uses evergreen.

### Neutral

- **Warm paper** (`paper`): page ground, form fields, comparison-number backing and browser theme.
- **Linen inset** (`surface`): shopping field, product illustration backings and unavailable-image placeholders.
- **Charcoal** (`ink`): primary copy, wordmark, active concept underline and dark button.
- **Secondary ink** (`muted`): descriptions, captions and ordinary price-status copy.
- **Control edge** (`control-border`): meaningful select boundaries and filter hover edges.
- **Quiet rule** (`line`): dividers, section borders and decorative grid separation.
- **Warm inverse** (`inverse`): recommendation heading, action and keyboard focus; selected text and the skip link.
- **Inverse secondary** (`inverse-muted`): recommendation paragraphs and secondary copy.
- **Inverse edge** (`inverse-border`): recommendation seal ring.

Text selection uses evergreen with warm inverse text. The recommendation explicitly supplies its inverse secondary text and focus roles. The sidecar's synthesized tonal strips are preview metadata; they are not additional shipped interface tokens.

## Typography

**Display font:** Cormorant Garamond, then Georgia and serif. **Utility/body font:** Source Sans 3, then Arial and sans-serif. Loaded faces are Cormorant Garamond normal 500 and italic 400; Source Sans 3 normal 400 and 600. Font synthesis is disabled. The normal display face and regular body face are preloaded locally; no external font service is needed.

## Layout

The current studio uses the family spread followed by the six-person portrait board, details, recommendation, comparison, shopping and preparation plan. The studio lookbook is centered with a 1280px maximum width; the wider page is capped at 1800px from 1600px upward. Its desktop scene has an image column capped at 850px and a palette column of at least 170px, separated by 24px. At ≤900px the scene stacks; the palette uses seven columns, then four at ≤600px. The studio lookbook uses 20px side padding at ≤600px.

Concept navigation has five columns on wider screens. At ≤700px it uses a six-track grid with the first three controls spanning two tracks and the last two spanning three. Controls remain visible. The portrait board changes from six to three columns at ≤1100px and to two at ≤700px. Shopping cards change from four to three to two columns at those same breakpoints. Preparation columns stack at ≤700px.

Standard lower sections use 70px vertical and 7% horizontal padding, reducing to 42px vertical at ≤700px. The spacing entries record recurring values rather than impose a new arithmetic scale. Existing shopping and study descriptions use maximum measures between 65ch and 75ch where declared. Images retain their established proportions; the complete family view is not cropped.

## Elevation & Depth

The live editorial studio is predominantly flat: paper, inset surfaces, hairline rules and photographic content provide depth. Selected concepts and portraits use an inset underline, and completed products an inset edge. Existing shopping illustrations retain a small garment drop shadow (`3px 5px 3px #34362b22`); it is illustration treatment, not a floating-card convention. The fallback illustrated hero retains its original paper and garment shadows, but it is hidden in the current studio layout.

## Shapes

Controls and editorial containers are square. Circular geometry belongs to swatch indicators, detail color dots and the recommendation seal; the small navigation count uses its existing rounded capsule. The recommendation ring is 115px across normally and 65px at ≤700px. The mobile monogram roles are sized for that unchanged circle.

## Components

### Buttons and links

Dark filled actions use ink backgrounds with paper-colored text and change to deep evergreen on fine-pointer hover. Text buttons remain transparent, underlined and at least 44px high. Action links use evergreen with an underline or top divider; ordinary text links have a 36px minimum height, while shopping links are at least 44px high. Pointer-only hover treatment is gated by hover capability. Pressed buttons scale to 0.98; reduced motion disables this transform.

### Inputs / Fields

Native status selects use paper, ink, a control-edge border, square corners and a 44px minimum height. Comparison selects use a bottom border. The type role stays at the body size. Focus is a two-pixel evergreen outline with a five-pixel offset; the dark recommendation uses warm inverse focus. Disabled buttons retain full opacity and muted text in the screen typography layer.

### Navigation

The masthead uses a serif wordmark, compact second line and sans-serif text links. On phones its links occupy a separate full-width row below a thin rule. The edition label is hidden at ≤1100px. Concept controls are serif labels with compact subtitles, square transparent fields and a persistent two-pixel ink underline for the active concept. Concept switching is immediate; intro and outfit-detail animation are disabled to avoid double-exposed faces.

### Chips and palette choices

Shopping filters are transparent rectangular buttons with an evergreen border when selected. Palette choices pair a clothing-color swatch with its name; current selection includes a contrasting dot and descriptive text. Palette colors remain content data. Their appearance is not a source for interface token changes.

### Cards / Containers

Portrait choices remain open, without enclosing cards; the active choice has a persistent inset underline. Product cards use flat inset illustration fields and an open text stack. Completed items add a one-pixel evergreen inset edge and retain their textual/checklist status. Shopping rows keep their established divider, thumbnail and native-select arrangement.

### Image publication

Public mode includes only reviewed, approved generated family visualizations and individual studies recorded in `content/published-assets.json`, alongside original shopping illustrations. Original photos and design mockups remain local. Generated imagery is always labeled as approximate AI wardrobe visualization. New imagery requires separate approval; publishing an approved copy makes that image public.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the five concepts, six individualized outfits per concept, existing section order, responsive grids and image framing.
- **Do** use the interface tokens independently from the wardrobe palette values in structured content.
- **Do** label generated images as approximate AI wardrobe visualizations and preserve complete family framing.
- **Do** keep concept switches immediate, selection persistent, visible keyboard focus and global reduced-motion support.
- **Do** preserve shopping facts, verification limits, safe external-link attributes and accessible labels.
- **Do** keep original photos and design mockups private; only the reviewed generated copies recorded in `content/published-assets.json` may be public.

### Don't:

- **Don't** reintroduce Manrope or synthesize missing font weights/styles.
- **Don't** use the subtle divider color as a substitute for a meaningful control boundary.
- **Don't** indicate selection with color alone or crossfade family images into double-exposed faces.
- **Don't** import, embed, hotlink, log, commit or deploy private original photos or reference HTML containing them.
- **Don't** publish new imagery without separate approval; an approved public website copy is public.
- **Don't** push, create a repository or deploy without the user's explicit instruction and review of what will be public.
