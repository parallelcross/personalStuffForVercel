# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
A family of six comparing three fall-photo wardrobes, choosing coordinated outfits and planning purchases.

## Confirmed constraints
React, TypeScript and Vite. Static production on Vercel with no backend, tracking or paid runtime services. Hill Country Heritage is recommended; Texas Organic and Austin Editorial remain distinct, with six complete outfits each. Shopping facts live in structured content and uncertain prices remain labeled estimates.

## Privacy
Original photos and design mockups remain private. On October 14, 2026, the user reviewed and approved deployment of the generated studio imagery. Only the optimized generated copies recorded in content/published-assets.json may be public. Future imagery requires separate approval.

## Brand commitments
Warm editorial fashion direction, refined typography, mobile usability and accessible controls. Real retailer photography only with permission; clearly labeled styling illustrations otherwise.

## Evidence
`content/wardrobe.json` contains the wardrobe plans and dated shopping verification. Private references and generated images are excluded from Git. Actual fit, stock and likeness require human review.
